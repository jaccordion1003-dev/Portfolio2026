Xuewei Qian — portfolio website

This iteration combines the supplied v2 opening screen with the portfolio content below it.
- index.html: animated first screen, five featured Bilibili video embeds and links, and the 55-image storyboard gallery
- about.html: retained as the destination for the About link
- styles.css: Plus Jakarta Sans, responsive layout, staggered text entrance, image styling, and difference-blend text
- hero-motion.js: shuffles the nine images on each page load, preserves their aspect ratios, follows pointer movement, and rapidly cycles through them
- assets/hero/: the nine images used in the opening animation, including the four newly added images
- assets/images/: the 55 numbered portfolio images, arranged in pairs with image 13 spanning a full row

The opening composition follows the 1920 × 975 artboard. The image animation respects the device's reduced-motion setting.
